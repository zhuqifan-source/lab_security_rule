IIARC Laboratory Safety Rules and Regulations

IIARC实验室安全及使用规定

A 24-hour monitoring system has been installed. Individuals shall bear full responsibility for any consequences arising from violations of the following regulations.

实验室已安装24小时监控系统。违反下列规定所造成的一切后果由个人负责。

1. All laboratory access shall be applied and authorized via the Laboratory Management Platform (LMP), and complete the required safety training, including but not limited to safety rules and regulations training and standard operation procedures (SOP) of equipment and operations training, and sign the health and safety agreement. All users shall obey all safety rules and regulations all the time.

1. 所有实验室的准入权限必须通过实验室管理平台（LMP）进行申请和授权，完成必要的安全培训，包括但不限于安全规章制度培训、设备和操作的标准作业程序（SOP）培训签署安全知情书等。每一个实验室的使用者都必须始终遵守所有的安全规章制度。

2. Only authorized personnel may enter and/or work in the laboratory. All laboratory doors shall be kept closed at all times.

2. 只有被授权的人可以进入实验室和/或在实验室工作。实验室的所有门都必须始终保持关闭状态。

3. Never work alone in the laboratory. Any laboratory work outside normal working hours requires an application for off-hours access via the Laboratory Management Platform (LMP). Access will only be granted upon approval.

3. 不允许单独在实验室里工作。非工作时间使用实验室需在实验室管理平台（LMP）中申请非常规时间准入，审批通过后，方可进入实验室。

4. It is forbidden to move or take out laboratory equipment, tools, and furniture without permission. Renting equipment, tools, or venues to others without permission is also prohibited.

4. 禁止私自挪动或带出实验室设备仪器和家具。禁止将设备仪器、场所私自租借他人使用。

5. Familiarize yourself with the location and usage of safety showers, eyewash stations, fire extinguishers, and first-aid kits.

5. 进入实验室前详细了解各类应急设备的位置，作用，应用场景和操作规范，例如：实验室安全喷淋、洗眼器、灭火器和急救箱。

6. Keep the aisle clear. Do not block access to emergency equipment or exits.

6. 保持过道畅通，禁止堵塞应急设备或出口的通道。

7. The laboratory is dedicated to experimental work only. No unnecessary staying or non‑experimental activities are permitted.

7. 实验室为实验专用场所，非实验相关人员不得无故逗留。禁止在实验室内开展与实验无关的活动。

8. No waste bins are provided inside the laboratory. All waste, including packaging cartons, must be cleared out of the lab promptly.

8. 实验室内不设置垃圾箱。所有废料（含快递纸箱等包装废弃物）须及时清理出实验室。

9. It is strictly prohibited to enter the laboratory after drinking alcohol or taking drowsy / psychotropic drugs; drinking in the experimental area is forbidden. Personnel under the influence of alcohol are not allowed to perform any experimental operations, equipment inspections or commissioning work.

9. 严禁酒后、服用嗜睡 / 精神类药物后进入实验室；严禁在实验区域饮酒。酒后人员禁止开展任何实验操作、设备巡检与调试工作。
