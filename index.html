IIARC Laboratory Safety Rules and Regulations

IIARC实验室安全及使用规定

A 24-hour monitoring system has been installed. Individuals are responsible for any consequences caused by violation of the below regulations.

实验室已安装24小时监控系统。违反下列规定所造成的一切后果由个人负责。

1. All laboratory access shall be applied and authorized via the Laboratory Management Platform (LMP), and complete the required safety training, including but not limited to safety rules and regulations training and standard operation procedures (SOP) of equipment and operations training, and sign the health and safety agreement. All users shall obey all safety rules and regulations all the time.

所有实验室的准入权限必须通过实验室管理平台（LMP）进行申请和授权，完成必要的安全培训，包括但不限于安全规章制度培训、设备和操作的标准操作程序（SOP）培训，签署安全知情书等。每一个实验室的使用者都必须始终遵守所有的安全规章制度。

2. Only authorized personnel may enter and/or work in the laboratory. All laboratory doors shall be kept closed at all times.

只有被授权的人员可以进入实验室和/或在实验室工作。实验室的所有门都必须始终保持关闭状态。

3. Never work alone in the laboratory. Any lab work off-normal working hours requires the application for non-working hours access in the Laboratory Management Platform system (LMP). Access to the laboratory is only granted after approval.

不允许单独在实验室里工作。非工作时间使用实验室需在实验室管理平台（LMP）中申请非常规时间准入，审批通过后，方可进入实验室。

4. It is forbidden to move or take out laboratory equipment, tools, and furniture without permission. Renting equipment, tools, or venues to others without permission is also prohibited.

禁止私自挪动或带出实验室设备、仪器和家具。禁止将设备、仪器或场所私自租借他人使用。

5. Familiarize yourself with the location and usage of safety showers, eyewash stations, fire extinguishers, and first aid boxes.

进入实验室前，应详细了解各类应急设备的位置、作用、应用场景和操作规范，例如实验室安全喷淋、洗眼器、灭火器和急救箱。

6. Keep the aisle clear. Do not block access to emergency equipment or exits.

保持过道畅通，禁止堵塞应急设备或出口的通道。

7. Appropriately wear personal protective equipment (PPE) as required and return all PPE when leaving. Long hair and loose clothing must be pulled back and secured from entanglement or potential capture.

进入实验室前需正确穿戴个人防护设备（PPE），有序整理个人仪容仪表。留长发者应束扎头发，离开实验室后应正确归还防护设备。

8. Never consume and/or store food or beverages in the laboratory. Never apply cosmetics in areas where hazardous chemicals are used or stored. Keep the laboratory clean and organized.

禁止在实验室食用和/或存放食品或饮料。禁止在使用或存放危险化学品的区域使用化妆品。保持实验室整洁、有序。

9. No horseplay will be tolerated.

禁止在实验室内喧哗和打闹。

10. All containers must have appropriate labels. Unlabeled chemicals should never be used.

所有实验室容器必须附有恰当的标识标签，禁止使用任何未经标注的化学品。

11. Obtain assessment and approval before using any open flames or heat sources in the lab.

任何形式的明火或热源在实验室使用前都必须经过评估和批准。

12. All users must be trained for using any of the equipment and tools. When using equipment and tools, SOP must be strictly followed.

所有使用者必须接受设备和工具的相关培训。使用设备和工具时，必须严格遵守标准操作程序（SOP）。

13. Do not use any extension cord without permission. Report all electrical faults, damaged sockets and plugs to the responsible technical staff. Do not try to repair any electrical faults by yourself. Any equipment not in use should be switched off.

未经许可不得使用任何电器延长线。如发现电气故障、插座或插头损坏，请及时向负责的技术人员报告，禁止自行尝试修复电气故障。任何未使用的设备都应关闭电源，以节约能源并降低火灾风险。

14. After completing your work, turn off all equipment, cut off water and gas supply, put things back in place, and clean the table.

每次实验结束后，关闭所有设备，切断水源和气源，将物品归位，并清理实验台面。

15. No person under the influence of alcohol, sedatives or psychotropic drugs shall enter the laboratory. Alcohol consumption is prohibited in laboratory areas. Personnel in an intoxicated state are not allowed to perform any experimental operations, equipment inspections or commissioning.

严禁酒后或服用具有镇静作用的药物、精神类药物后进入实验室；严禁在实验区域饮酒。处于醉酒状态的人员禁止开展任何实验操作、设备巡检与调试工作。

16. Do not leave any personal belongings behind. The center is not responsible for any loss or damage to personal items left in the laboratory.

请勿遗留任何个人物品。因个人物品遗留在实验室而造成的丢失或损坏，中心不承担任何责任。

17. For any questions about hardware or software, please report to the lab technician team.

如有任何设备软硬件方面的问题，请及时告知实验室技术支持团队。

18. Report any accidents, injuries and spills to relevant supervisor and technical staff immediately.

发生任何意外事故、人员受伤或泄漏事件时，应立即向相关主管和技术支持人员报告。

19. Do not pour chemicals down drains. Chemical wastes shall be disposed as required by the University safety regulations.

禁止直接将化学品排入下水道。化学废物应严格按照学校的安全规定进行处理。

20. No public waste bins are provided in the laboratory. All experimental waste, as well as sundries such as express packaging boxes generated during equipment entry, must be promptly removed and disposed of. Storage and stacking inside the laboratory are strictly prohibited.

实验室不设置公共垃圾箱。所有实验废弃物，以及设备进场产生的快递包装箱等杂物，须及时清运处置，严禁在实验室内留存或堆放。

21. The laboratory is a dedicated experimental area. Personnel not involved in experiments shall not stay without valid reasons. Activities unrelated to experiments are prohibited in the laboratory.

中心实验室为实验专用场所，非实验相关人员不得无故逗留。禁止在实验室内开展与实验无关的活动。

Violations will lead to disciplinary consequences.

违反上述规定者，将会承担相应后果。

违规行为由实验员日常检查记录，监控抽查发现违规时须及时留存证据。IIARC中心实行以下分级处罚：首次违规：通过邮件警告。二次违规：暂停门禁7–14天，时长视违规情节而定；违规人员须限期完成安全反思，并通过期满评估方可恢复门禁，未通过则延长封禁。三次违规：立即取消门禁并禁止进入实验室；重新申请准入并完成相关安全培训后，方可获得实验室使用资格。

Violations shall be recorded by lab technicians during routine inspections, with evidence promptly preserved for violations identified through surveillance spot checks. The IIARC Centre shall apply the following disciplinary measures: First Violation: A warning shall be issued via email. Second Violation: Lab access shall be suspended for 7–14 days, depending on the severity of the violation. The violator must complete a safety reflection within the specified timeframe, and access may be restored only after passing the end-of-suspension assessment; otherwise, the suspension shall be extended. Third Violation: Lab access shall be revoked immediately, and entry to the laboratory shall be prohibited. A new access application and completion of required safety training are necessary to regain eligibility for laboratory access.
